*Talk to the land owner*
For a source to sea walk, the Ouse Valley Way starts with a rather deflating sign.

<img src="/assets/images/os_source_sign.jpg" class="rounded card-img-top" alt="...">

So where is the *actual* source?

The blue line on the map heads towards Wappenham.

<img src="/assets/images/os_source_2.jpg" class="rounded card-img-top" alt="...">

There is a way of getting there by pootpaths, but I found it easiest heading up the road until I got to the nearest footpath to Wappenham Lodge Farm. The path goes past the farm house through fields of sheep to a fenced off pond.

<img src="/assets/images/os_source_2.jpg" class="rounded card-img-top" alt="...">

The little blue line that is the next field but without water - just plants that indicate water below the surface.

## Ouse Well
Near Fathinghoe and furthest as the crow flies from the Wash
There's a footpath nearby but the actual source is only accessible via a copse on private land.
