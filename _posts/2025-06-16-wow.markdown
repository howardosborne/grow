20th July is WOW! Day (Walk the Ouse valley Way) when the Great Ouse's greatest path is celebrated. Walk along the path, collect three stamps along the way and you'll receive a free t-shirt.

There will be information hubs along the way at these points:

- Earith The Crystal Ship (Papworth Brewery)
- Holywell The Old Ferryboat Inn
- St Ives Norris Museum
- Houghton St Mary’s Centre, Chapel Lane
- Godmanchester Chinese Bridge
- Brampton Brampton Mill
- Buckden Buckden Marina 
- Paxton Pits Visitor Centre
- St Neots Riverside Park 

[details](https://greatousevalleytrust.org.uk/walk-the-ouse-valley-way/)
