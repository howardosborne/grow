Hi, I'm Howard and I like water. If there's a river, lake or sea nearby, I want to be in it.

Over the last few years I've been learning more about my local river, the Great Ouse, and the places and people who also live by it. It's encouraged me to take part by helping set up the [Great Ouse Rivers Trust](https://www.greatouseriverstrust.org). I've still much more to learn and if you can help me, please [get in touch](https://forms.gle/QDaxuyay3GvBgKMo7) 

### Reading
Here are some of the books that I have found useful:
- Great Ouse Country by Andrew Hunter Blair
- Ouse's Silent Tide by Rev. C.F. Farrar
- The Great Ouse by Wilson Stephens
- The River Great Ouse and the River Cam by Joseph Jeremiah
- The Buildings of England: Buckinghamshire by Niklaus Pevsner
- The Buildings of England: Bedfordshire, Huntingdon and Peterborough by Niklaus Pevsner
- The Buildings of England: Cambridgeshire by Niklaus Pevsner
- Harris's Guide to Churches and Cathedrals by Brian L. Harris
- Church Going by Andrew Ziminski
- Newport Pagnell's Bridges by Dennin C. Mynard, Paul Woodfield and Ray Bailey
