There are some great resources for paddling the Great Ouse.

[Go Paddling](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/river-great-ouse/) offers a one-stop-shop for where to paddle and useful tips from other paddlers. 
They also offer guides for paddling the principle fenland drains: The [Old Bedford River](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/old-bedford-river/) and [New Bedford River](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/new-bedford-river/)

The [River Obstacles Database](https://river-obstacles-theriverstrust.hub.arcgis.com/) is a definitive resource for things that will get in your way.

If you are looking to hire a canoe in the Milton Keynes area, then [Canoe the Ouse](https://www.canoetheouse.co.uk/) is worth contacting.

For Bedford, try [Canoe Trail](https://www.canoetrail.co.uk/canoe-kayak-sup-hire/canoe-hire).

The National Trust have prodiced some guidance on canoeing from Houghton Mill: [Canoeing from Houghton Mill](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/houghton-mill-and-waterclose-meadows/canoeing-from-houghton-mill-to-st-ives)
