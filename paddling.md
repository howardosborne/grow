There are some great resources for paddling the Great Ouse.

## Go Paddling
[Go Paddling](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/river-great-ouse/){:target="_blank"} offers a one-stop-shop for where to paddle and useful tips from other paddlers as well as buying membership which covers permits and insurance.

They also offer guides for paddling the principle fenland drains:

[Old Bedford River guide](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/old-bedford-river/){:target="_blank"} 

[New Bedford River guide](https://gopaddling.info/rivers/new-bedford-river/){:target="_blank"}

## River Obstacles Database
The [River Obstacles Database](https://river-obstacles-theriverstrust.hub.arcgis.com/){:target="_blank"} is a definitive resource for things that will get in your way.

## Hiring a canoe
If you are looking to hire a canoe in the Milton Keynes area, then [Canoe the Ouse](https://www.canoetheouse.co.uk/){:target="_blank"} is worth contacting.

For Bedford, try [Canoe Trail](https://www.canoetrail.co.uk/canoe-kayak-sup-hire/canoe-hire){:target="_blank"}.

## Reporting pollution 
It's not nice to think about the sorts of things that could have ended up in the river, but if you see (or smell) a potential source of pollution then report it to the Environment Agency.

Environment Agency Hotline: 0800 807060

[Environment Agency](https://www.gov.uk/report-environmental-problem)

## Houghton Mill
The National Trust have prodiced some guidance on [canoeing from Houghton Mill](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cambridgeshire/houghton-mill-and-waterclose-meadows/canoeing-from-houghton-mill-to-st-ives){:target="_blank"}
