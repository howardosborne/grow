The first leg of the Ouse Valley Way skirts around Stowe Gardens which is a, if not *the*, quintessential English Landscape Garden.

They were designed and nutured by gardening gurus William Kent and Capability Brown back in the 18th Century and are now owned and run by the National Trust. I met with Head Ranger Barry Smith to find out more.
