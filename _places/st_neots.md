St Neots is a town of around 30,000 inhabitants. It includes Eaton Socon and Eaton Ford on the west bank of the river.

The river is a well used highlight of the town(s) with pleasant parkland setting the scene.

### Getting there
The 905 bus goes between Bedford and Cambridge via [St Neots](./st-neots.html) as well as [Great Barford](./great-barford.html), Roxton, Wyboston and Eaton Socon.

Getting to/from Godmanchester is best done via adjoining Huntingdon which has a railway station with regular trains to St Neots.
[departures](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/live-trains/departures/st-neots/)

The No. 66 bus also connects Huntingdon with St Neots via Buckden and on to Cambridge.
[bus timetables](https://www.stagecoachbus.com/)
