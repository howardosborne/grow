### getting there
The 'B the busway' connects with Huntingdon (adjoining Godmanchester) via Houghton, which is on the route.
In the other direction, the 301 connects to [Earith](./earith.md) via Needingworth (for [Holywell](./holywell.md))
Getting to/from Ely means taking a bus to Cambridge (either A or B the busway) and then a train or bus to Ely.
