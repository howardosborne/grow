The village of Sharnbrook, named after the Sharn Brook which runs along the edge of the village, can thank the local limestone for its looks.

It's also home to Unilever R&D which for a while had the [world's most northerly tea plantation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150402101034/http://www.unilever.com.uy/Images/iv-icn09_About-the-Lipton-Institute-of-Tea_tcm186-189709.pdf)

For food and drink, the [Swan with two Nicks](https://www.swanwithtwonicks.com/index) is reasonably priced.

### Accommodation
There are a couple of options for overnighting. [The Mill](https://www.themillsharnbrook.co.uk/) and the [Riverside Lodge](https://www.riversidelodgebedford.co.uk/).

### Getting there
Both the Nos. 25 and 50 buses go to [Bedford](./bedford.html) with the No 25 going via Harrold and Stevington.
