Harrold is an attractive limestone village of around 1,500 people and it's well worth a wander around.

Harrold bridge is a popular put-in point for kayak and paddleboarders while the adjoining Country Park offers walks around the former gravel pit turned nature reserve.

### Food and drink
Harrold has two pubs, the Muntjac, which is recommended for curry, and [the Oakley Arms](http://www.theoakleyharrold.co.uk/), which apparently is where Paul McCartney [first sang Hey Jude](https://www.bedfordshirelive.co.uk/news/bedfordshire-news/dad-remembers-magical-night-paul-4997061).

There's also a Co-op for general provisions.

### Accommodation
There a several places registered with [airbnb](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/s/Harrold--Bedford) and it's also worth a search on [booking.com](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.en-gb.html?ss=Harrold%2C+Bedfordshire%2C)

### Getting there
The No 25 bus from Harrold goes to Bedford via Stevington and [Pavenham](./pavenham.html), which is on the walking route.
