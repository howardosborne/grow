### Accommodation
The Old Ferry Boat is an ideal location, being directly on the route. You can find this and other alternatives at [booking.com](https://www.booking.com/searchresults.en-gb.html?ss=Needingworth)

### Getting there
Nearest bus stop is at Needingworth where you can catch a 301 to [Earith](./earith.html) or [St Ives](./st-ives.html).
